# Design System: Antons Blocked Drains Hawkesbury

Creative direction: a drainage field manual built from the Antons vehicle livery and real job evidence.

- Deep navy is the working surface; clear blue identifies drainage information; red is reserved for direct phone actions.
- Saira Extra Condensed carries short, forceful service headings. Barlow carries readable explanations.
- Square controls, ruled lists and full-bleed field photography communicate capability without soft marketing cards.
- The first viewport pairs a symptom-led promise with a technician actively using drain equipment.
- Real CCTV, jetting and excavation media must do the authority work. Avoid stock people, gradients, glass effects and invented badges.
- Desktop uses split fields and asymmetrical information. Mobile stacks into one clear path with a persistent call control.
- Motion stays restrained and respects reduced motion; speed and content stability outrank decorative animation.
